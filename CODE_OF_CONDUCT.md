# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Smart Shading contributors and maintainers are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing evidence and impact clearly.
- Keep reviews focused on the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy and remove installation-specific or personal data from reports.
- Accept reasonable maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and project direction.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Security details and credentials must not be posted publicly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, consistent, and focused on protecting the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through GitHub when possible. If that is not safe or practical, use GitHub's abuse-reporting tools. Do not open a public issue containing private conduct details.

This Code of Conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces, and when someone officially represents the project.
